I'm a global B2B marketing executive with 20+ years of experience leading demand generation, go-to-market strategy, partner marketing, and field execution across enterprise SaaS, cybersecurity, and mission-critical technology organizations.

My focus is simple: align marketing directly to revenue.

I've built and scaled global marketing programs in both high-growth and publicly traded environments, partnering closely with sales, product, SDRs, and executive leadership to drive pipeline creation, acceleration, and expansion. My experience spans enterprise software, cloud-based platforms, cybersecurity, and secure infrastructure solutions serving Fortune 500, public safety, government, and commercial customers.

I take a data-driven, customer-centric approach to marketing, leveraging automation, analytics, and cross-functional alignment to create repeatable, scalable programs that fuel sustainable growth.

I've led multi-million dollar budgets, launched global campaigns, built ABM frameworks, scaled partner programs, executed 80+ industry events, and driven measurable pipeline impact across regions including North America, EMEA, and APAC.

My leadership style is strategic but hands-on. I believe in clarity, accountability, and building high-performing teams that know how to move fast without breaking the brand.

The work is fractional CMO leadership, strategic growth consulting, and revenue marketing advisory. I build the plan, and I stay for the execution.

Great marketing isn't about noise. It's about creating momentum that sales can feel.
