About

I build marketing engines that drive measurable revenue growth.

I'm a global B2B marketing executive with 20+ years of experience leading demand generation, go-to-market strategy, partner marketing, and field execution across enterprise SaaS, cybersecurity, and mission-critical technology organizations.

My focus is simple: align marketing directly to revenue.

I've built and scaled global marketing programs in both high-growth and publicly traded environments, partnering closely with sales, product, SDRs, and executive leadership to drive pipeline creation, acceleration, and expansion. My experience spans enterprise software, cloud-based platforms, cybersecurity, and secure infrastructure solutions serving Fortune 500, public safety, government, and commercial customers.
